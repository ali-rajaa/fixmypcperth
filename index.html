Why Is My Computer So Slow? The Warning Signs Perth Users Ignore

"Why is my computer so slow" and "why is my PC so slow" are two of the most common things people search in Perth before they book a repair. What most people don't realise is that a slow computer is rarely just an annoyance — it's usually the first visible symptom of something failing underneath. Here are the signs we see most often in the Canning Vale workshop, and what each one actually means for your machine:

  • Boot times creeping past a minute or two. This is the classic early sign of a mechanical hard drive nearing the end of its life. Drives rarely fail cleanly — they slow down first, then take your files with them.
  • Freezing, stuttering or "Not Responding" during normal use. Often memory or storage-related, and it tends to worsen quickly once it starts. Every additional day of use writes more data to a drive that's already struggling.
  • The fans running loud and constant, or the case hot to touch. Dust build-up forces your CPU to throttle itself to survive. Sustained heat permanently shortens the life of the processor, drive and motherboard — and Perth summers make this significantly worse.
  • Browsing feels sluggish even when nothing is open. Frequently a sign of malware or adware running quietly in the background. The longer it runs undetected, the more it harvests — passwords, banking logins, saved card details.
  • Programs crashing, files refusing to open, or documents appearing corrupted. This is late-stage. At this point data loss is no longer hypothetical, and recovery gets harder and more expensive with every startup.
  • Blue screens, random restarts or the machine shutting itself down. Hardware-level instability. Continuing to run a computer in this state is the single most common reason a recoverable repair becomes an unrecoverable one.
  • Windows taking longer and longer to update, or updates failing outright. Usually corrupted system files. Unpatched systems are also the ones that get hit hardest by ransomware.

The pattern we see over and over: people live with a slow computer for months, then bring it in the week it finally dies — when what would have been a straightforward same-day repair has turned into a data recovery job. If your PC or laptop is showing any of the signs above, the cheapest and safest time to have it looked at is now, while it still turns on. Our diagnostic tells you exactly what's happening before you commit to anything, and most jobs booked before 2pm are back in your hands the same day.

Computer Virus Removal Perth: Signs Your PC Is Infected

Searches for computer virus removal Perth and "how do I know if my computer has a virus" spike constantly, and for good reason — modern malware is designed to stay hidden while it works. Common signs include: your browser homepage or search engine changed without you doing it, new toolbars or extensions you didn't install, pop-ups appearing even when you're not browsing, programs opening or closing on their own, your antivirus being disabled without your permission, and friends receiving strange messages or emails "from you" that you never sent.

Every day an infection stays on your machine, it keeps working. Modern malware logs keystrokes, harvests saved passwords, captures banking and card details, and quietly spreads to other devices on your home or office network. Ransomware variants sit dormant for weeks before encrypting everything at once. If you're seeing any of the signs above, the machine should be looked at now — not after the weekend.

Our virus and malware removal service in Perth starts from $80 and covers a full system scan, removal of viruses, spyware, ransomware and browser hijackers, a full browser cleanup, and setting up proper antivirus protection so it doesn't come straight back. Data Recovery Perth: The Warning Signs You Cannot Afford to Ignore

If you're searching data recovery Perth or "signs your hard drive is failing," treat it as urgent. A failing drive gets worse every single time it spins up, and the window where your files are still recoverable closes fast. The warning signs to look for: clicking, grinding or beeping noises from the machine, files that suddenly won't open or appear corrupted, the computer freezing during file transfers, folders that appear empty when you know they aren't, and increasingly frequent blue screen crashes.

If your drive is making noise, power the machine off and bring it in. The most heartbreaking jobs we see are the ones where someone kept restarting a clicking drive for a fortnight hoping it would sort itself out, and by the time it reached us the platters were beyond reach. Caught early, recovery is routine. Caught late, sometimes nothing can be done at any price.
