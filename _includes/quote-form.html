One copy for the service pages, /services, Tech Tips, suburb pages and /quote-request, so every enquiry arrives in the same shape: Services, Device Type, Description, Name, Phone or Email, Preferred Contact, Suburb (suburb pages), from_page. Styles: assets/css/form.css. Built from conversion research (see STATUS.md, Quote forms): - taps first, typing last: what it is and what kind of computer are buttons, never dropdowns - one contact field: the reply choice decides whether we ask for a mobile or an email, so nobody types both - one optional field, marked Optional - the button says what they get, in their words ("Get my fixed price") - after a send, one tap carries on in WhatsApp, where a photo can go Reads (all optional): include.title heading, else "Get a fixed price" include.desc line under the heading page.quote_service the service this page is about. When set, the "What do you need help with?" buttons are skipped and the value is sent hidden. page.default_device device button ticked on arrival page.suburb sent as "Suburb" on suburb pages ?service= in the URL (a quote_options key) ticks that service. The reply time comes from _config.yml.
