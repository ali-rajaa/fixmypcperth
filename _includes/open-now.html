The whole bar is a link to directions. The script runs straight after the header, before the page content is parsed, so the content is laid out below the bar from the start and nothing moves when it appears. It places the bar just under whichever header is showing and re-places it on resize, because some pages pad the body for the phone header, some pad their hero instead, and one pads the body 4px more than the header is tall. It scrolls away with the page. A minute check adds it at 8pm and takes it away at midnight for anyone with a page left open. No JavaScript, no bar.
