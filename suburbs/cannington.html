why_local: |

Cannington has been settled since the 1840s, but it has never been busier than it is now. Its population more than doubled between 2001 and 2021, reaching about 6,900 people in just four square kilometres, and the median age is around 30, one of the youngest in the area.

The suburb revolves around Westfield Carousel, which opened in 1972 and has grown through two major expansions into one of Perth's largest shopping centres. Albany Highway runs through the western side with its mix of shops, small warehouses and the City of Canning offices. Around both, a lot of the housing is units and apartments rather than family homes on big blocks.

That makes Cannington a suburb of renters, students and people early in their working lives, and it changes what the computers look like. Plenty are bought second-hand or at the budget end of the range, and laptops get carried on the train, to college and to work. The problems follow: hinges that crack, trackpads that stop responding, and cheap new laptops that feel slow the day they come out of the box.

In Cannington the laptop tends to do everything: study, work, streaming and every bill and booking. And because so many residents commute and study away from home, it travels constantly. A laptop that goes back and forth on the train every day wears out in particular places, above all the hinges and the trackpad, the two parts that move and get handled most.

Both are worth fixing early. A cracked hinge puts strain on the screen cable every time the lid opens, and if it is left long enough it can take the display with it. A trackpad fault is often cheaper to fix than people expect, since it is sometimes a driver rather than hardware.

If you are thinking of buying a used laptop, bring it in before you hand over the money. A short check tells you whether the battery, drive and screen are as good as the listing claims.

desktops_intro: |

Desktops are less common in Cannington's units and apartments, where space is tight and a laptop does the job. The ones that are here tend to be gaming PCs or a compact machine for working from home, and they are usually worth keeping going rather than replacing.

For the small businesses along Albany Highway, the desktop behind the counter is often the one machine that runs the till, the email and the accounts. It is also usually the one nobody has looked at in years, right up until the day it will not start. A service and a check of the backups is cheap insurance for a computer the shop cannot trade without.

data_intro: |

Renters move more often, and every move is a chance for something to go missing: a laptop dropped in the shuffle, an external drive left in a box, a phone reset without its photos backed up first. Students keep years of coursework on one machine that goes everywhere with them.

Before a move, and before a laptop is sold or handed on, it is worth making sure everything important exists in more than one place. It takes minutes when nothing is wrong, and it is the difference between a lost laptop being an inconvenience and it being a loss.
