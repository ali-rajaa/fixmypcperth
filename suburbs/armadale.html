desktops_intro: |

Armadale's older residential streets carry a genuine share of desktops that have been in the same house for a decade or more, running on a mechanical drive that has slowed the whole machine down without anyone quite clocking how much. Migrating to an SSD is usually the single biggest change available, at a fraction of the cost of replacing the machine.

Heat is the other recurring desktop fault, particularly on towers that have sat collecting dust for years. Once the thermal paste dries out and the heatsink clogs, the machine starts cutting out under load. A clean and fresh paste generally sorts it out in an afternoon.

Given the distance, on-site visits across Armadale are usually reserved for business machines that cannot leave the premises, with remote support covering everything else that does not need hands physically on the computer.

data_intro: |

Some of the hardest calls we take are from people who assumed their files were safe until a drive proved otherwise, family photos, years of records for a local business. What can be recovered depends entirely on the drive's condition. Still spinning but struggling gives good odds, particularly if it is imaged early. Stopped completely is a harder job, and the odds drop from there.

Either way, the single best thing you can do is stop using the machine as soon as files start going missing. Continuing to run it is what turns a recoverable drive into one that is not.
