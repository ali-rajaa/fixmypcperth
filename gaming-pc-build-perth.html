A gaming PC that crashes under load but seems fine on the desktop is one of the most common jobs we see. The usual causes are a graphics driver that clashes with an update, memory that is unstable at its rated speed, a power supply that cannot keep up at full load, or a component overheating.

We test each one under load until the crash repeats, then fix the cause, whether that is a driver, a BIOS setting, a memory profile or a part. The all-in diagnostic covers finding it and the repair labour. If a part needs replacing, we tell you which and what it costs before fitting it.

- id: stutter group: "Gaming PC repair" title: "Stutter, low FPS and poor performance" price_key: diagnostic-repair-all-in signs: - "Lower frame rates than it used to get" - "Stutter or hitching in games" - "Graphics card or processor running below spec" - "Performance dropped after a few minutes" text: |

When a gaming PC performs below what its parts should manage, something is holding it back. It is often heat, with the processor or graphics card slowing itself down to stay cool, but it can also be memory running below its rated speed, the wrong power settings, a game installed on a slow drive, or background software.

We benchmark it, find the bottleneck and fix it, then benchmark again, so you can see the difference in numbers.

- id: overheating group: "Gaming PC repair" title: "Overheating, fan noise and repaste" price_key: cpu-thermal-paste-replacement also_keys: - overheating-fix-deep-clean signs: - "Fans ramping up loud during games" - "Temperatures climbing until FPS drops" - "PC shutting down mid-game" - "Dust visible in the filters and fans" text: |

Dust clogs filters and heatsinks, and thermal paste dries out over a few years. The result is a PC that gets louder and slower as it heats up, and in the worst cases shuts itself off. Perth summers make it worse.

A repaste removes the cooler, cleans both surfaces and applies premium thermal compound, which often drops temperatures a long way. For a PC that also needs a full dust-out, the deep clean covers the whole system.

- id: gpu-problems group: "Gaming PC repair" title: "Graphics card problems" price_key: diagnostic-repair-all-in also_keys: - gpu-install-driver-setup signs: - "Artefacts, flickering or coloured blocks" - "No display from the graphics card" - "Driver keeps crashing or recovering" - "Card fans not spinning" text: |

Graphics card faults range from a corrupted driver to a card that has failed. We test the card in the PC and on its own, check it is seated and powered properly, and clean-install the latest Nvidia or AMD drivers. Many "dead" graphics cards turn out to be a driver, a power cable or a slot problem.

If the card itself has failed, we tell you, and fit a replacement if you want one. We do not do component-level repair on graphics card boards.
