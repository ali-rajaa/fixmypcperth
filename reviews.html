Canning Vale workshop." --- {%- comment -%} Rebuilt from the old /reviews page. The rating is no longer written into this page. It comes from _data/reviews.yml, because a rating changes and a number typed into copy goes stale the moment it does. What was wrong with the old version: 1. It claimed "5.0 star rating across 200+ verified reviews" in the body copy, in the title tag, and as an AggregateRating schema block. The Google Business Profile is 4.8 from about 37. That is checkable in one click, and the schema version is worse than the copy version. 2. The AggregateRating block is gone entirely and is not coming back. Google discounts self-serving review markup a business puts on its own site about itself, and it is a documented cause of manual actions. The reviews below are presented as customer quotes, which is what they are. 3. "Perth's most trusted PC repair service" was an H2. It is a superlative with nothing behind it. Version 2 fixed the review carousel, which never worked: 4. Each review was wrapped in a slide that was itself a three column grid, so one card filled a third of the slide and two thirds sat empty. 5. The script scrolled the track and read track.scrollLeft, but the track was not a scroll container, so nothing moved and no dot updated. The rail is a real overflow-x scroller with snap points now. Native scrolling gives 1:1 finger tracking, momentum on release and a flick that can be caught and reversed mid motion. With JavaScript off the rail still scrolls; only the dots go missing. 6. Section headings were spans styled to look like headings, so the page had an H1 and no H2s. They are real h2.s-title elements now. Version 3, the top of the page: 7. Three FAQ entries had a price include written into _data/reviews.yml. Jekyll does not run Liquid inside data files, so those rendered as literal characters on the page and, worse, went into the FAQPage JSON-LD as literal characters too. They are [[service-key]] tokens now, resolved by _includes/resolve-prices.html at the point of output, so the figure still comes from services.yml and still cannot go stale. 8. The hero was a headline and a paragraph. The proof, a rating the whole page is named after, sat in a band below it as a bare number with no stars, and a visitor had to scroll before seeing a single review. The rating is now part of the hero with real star geometry, and one review sits beside it, so the page opens on the thing it is about. The featured review is the first entry in the data file and the rail below starts from the second, so no quote is printed twice on one screen. 9. The dark stats band is gone. Two of its three figures were the rating and the review count, which now sit in the hero, so it was repeating itself. The warranty line moved up with them. 10. Stars were the star character, which renders in whatever the system font happens to be, at whatever weight that font draws. They are one SVG symbol now, defined once and referenced, with the fill clipped to the exact rating, so 4.8 reads as 4.8 instead of rounding to five. 11. Suburb names came off the review cards. A first name and a suburb together points at a real person more precisely than a review needs to, and it read oddly. The featured review keeps the service it was for; the rail cards already show the service at the top. The suburb field stays in _data/reviews.yml, just unused. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign rv = site.data.reviews -%} {%- assign featured = rv.reviews | first -%} {%- comment -%} One star, defined once and referenced everywhere with .
