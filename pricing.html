Common PC and Laptop Problems We Fix in Perth

If your computer is showing any of the signs below, do not ignore them. Most faults are far cheaper to resolve when caught early, and every one of these is covered by upfront pricing.

Slow computer that takes forever to load

A PC that crawls is usually an ageing hard drive, too little RAM, or software clogging up startup. An SSD upgrade transforms most machines within an hour, and customers are consistently amazed how fast a five-year-old laptop can feel afterwards.

Random shutdowns and blue screen errors

Blue screens and unexpected restarts mean something needs attention. Common causes are failing RAM, overheating, driver conflicts or a corrupted Windows install. All are covered by our $120 all-in diagnostic and repair fee.

Viruses, pop-ups and browser hijacking

If your browser redirects to strange sites, shows constant pop-ups or your antivirus has vanished, you almost certainly have malware. Our $99 removal service eliminates it and sets up protection against reinfection, usually same day.

Computer won't turn on or boot past the logo

A machine that will not power on, sticks on the manufacturer logo or loops through restarts needs a proper hardware and software diagnostic. We identify the exact fault before quoting, so you always know what you are paying for.

Laptop overheating and fan running loud

Laptops are prone to heat issues as dust builds up and thermal paste dries out. If the fan runs constantly or the machine shuts off under load, a deep clean and repaste almost always resolves it and extends the machine's life.

Broken laptop screen or keyboard

A cracked screen or dead keys does not mean you need a new laptop. We replace screens and keyboards on most major brands, and it typically costs less than a third of the price of a replacement machine.

Fixed-scope jobs like virus removal at $99 or a password reset at $79 have their own set price. Parts are quoted separately and confirmed with you before anything is ordered.

Data recovery: act quickly for the best outcome

Losing access to your photos, documents and work files is one of the most stressful things that can happen with a computer. If your drive is clicking, has stopped being recognised by Windows, or the machine will not boot, there is a good chance the data is still recoverable — provided you stop using the device immediately. Every time a failing drive is accessed you risk overwriting data that could otherwise be saved. Recovery from a failing drive starts at $149. For completely dead drives that will not spin or mount, we operate a strict no fix, no fee policy: the $249 fee applies only if we succeed.

SSD upgrades: the best value upgrade there is

If your computer runs on an old spinning hard drive, an SSD upgrade is the single most cost-effective improvement available. A five-year-old laptop that takes four minutes to reach the desktop can start in under twenty seconds with the same processor, the same RAM and the same screen. We fit the drive and transfer your entire Windows installation, applications and personal files across, so nothing is lost. Labour alone is $65 if you supply the drive, or from $150 with a quality Samsung or Crucial drive supplied and fitted.
