How our pricing works

The rules behind the numbers

The {% include price.html key='diagnostic-repair-all-in' bare=true raw=true %} is the job, not the hour

One flat fee covers the diagnostic, all repair labour and the 30-day warranty. It is charged in full whether you go ahead with the repair or not, because the diagnostic is the work. If a part is needed, the labour to fit it is already included and you pay only for the part. Our guide to repair pricing covers why this costs less than hourly billing in the long run.

Prices are fixed, never requoted

The price you are quoted is the price you pay. If we find something else once the machine is open, we tell you. We do not come back with a bigger number.

Quotes hold for six months

A written quote from us is valid for six months from the date we issue it.

No fix no fee on data recovery

Both {% assign _dr = site.data.suburbs.service_links | where: "slug", "data-recovery-perth" | first %}{% if _dr.built %}data recovery{% else %}data recovery{% endif %} services carry it. On-site is a flat {% include price.html key='on-site-call-out' bare=true raw=true %} call-out

Charged per visit, on top of the service price, on every service. A $90 job at your place is $90 plus the {% include price.html key="on-site-call-out" bare=true %} call-out. Most mobile technicians in Perth bill $205 an hour with a one-hour minimum.

Everything includes GST

Parts and labour. No fees added at collection.

Payment

Cash, bank transfer or PayID. A deposit covering the part cost is taken when we have to order one, with the labour paid on collection.

Same day where we can

Drop off before 2pm and most repairs are ready by 6pm the same day at no extra cost. It is best effort rather than a guarantee, and we tell you early if we cannot make it. Work finishing after 6pm carries the {% include price.html key="priority-same-day-booking" bare=true %} priority booking fee.
