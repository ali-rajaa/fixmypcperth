Find your repair by what the computer is doing

Most people know what the computer is doing, not why. Start from the symptom and the right page is usually clear.

If none of these fit, or it is a mix of several, that is what the diagnostic is for. One flat fee finds the fault and covers the repair labour.

  1. 01

    Nothing happens when you press the power button.

    No lights, no fans, or lights that come on and then nothing. The cause is often the power supply, the charger or charging port, the motherboard or a stuck power state. See computer won't turn on.

  2. 02

    It turns on but never reaches Windows.

    Endless spinning dots, a repair loop or a black screen with a cursor. This is usually Windows itself or a failing drive. Windows reinstall covers the software side, and if the drive is clicking or very slow, read data recovery first so your files come off before anything is wiped.

  3. 03

    Blue screens or random restarts.

    These point to drivers, memory, a drive starting to fail or overheating. The all-in diagnostic finds which one, and laptop repair explains the laptop-specific causes.

  4. 04

    Everything takes forever.

    Slow start-up, programs that hang, a fan that never stops. An old hard drive, too little memory, a crowded startup list or dust in the cooling are the usual reasons. See slow computer repair, and computer upgrades if the hardware is the limit.

  5. 05

    Pop-ups, a changed homepage or a scam warning.

    Browser hijacks, fake virus alerts and remote-access scams all land here, as do hacked email and social accounts. See virus removal.

  6. 06

    Cracked, dark or striped laptop screen.

    A crack, dead patches, coloured lines or a screen that only works at certain angles. See laptop screen repair.

  7. 07

    Battery, keyboard, hinge or charging problems.

    A battery that dies in an hour, keys that stop working, a hinge that cracks the case or a charger that only works at an angle. See laptop repair.

  8. 08

    Files you cannot get to.

    A drive that is not detected, an external drive that clicks, deleted photos or a computer that will not start with your only copy inside. See data recovery, and stop using the drive until then.

  9. 09

    Games stutter, or you want a new build.

    Low frame rates, high temperatures, a build that will not post, or parts in boxes waiting to be put together. See gaming PC builds.

  10. 10

    Wifi, printers and the home network.

    Dead spots, a new NBN router, a printer that will not connect. These are done at your place, and the prices are under networking below.

How a repair works

  1. 1

    Message us

    Message us on WhatsApp or use the form below. You get a fixed price before you bring it in.

  2. 2

    Drop it off

    At our Canning Vale workshop, any time, day or night. Drop off before 2pm and most jobs are ready by 6pm the same day.

  3. 3

    Collect it fixed

    Tested before you collect it, with a 30-day warranty on the work.

A short message is enough to start: what the computer is, what it is doing and when it started. A photo of an error message or a cracked screen helps. We usually reply {{ site.reply_time }} with a price, or with one or two questions if the symptom could mean more than one thing.

Once it is on the bench, we confirm the fault before touching anything. If what we find matches the quote, we go ahead. If it turns out to be something different, we message you with the new price and wait for your answer. Every repair is tested before it goes back to you.

How our pricing works

All prices include GST. The complete list, with packages and current offers, is on the pricing page.

  • A fixed price, agreed first.

    Every job is quoted before work starts, and the price you agree is the price you pay at collection. There is no hourly rate running in the background.

  • One fee when the fault is unknown.

    When nobody can say yet what is wrong, the all-in diagnostic at {% include price.html key="diagnostic-repair-all-in" bare=true %} finds the fault and covers the repair labour in the same visit. You pay one figure for both, not a diagnostic fee followed by a repair bill.

  • Labour and parts are shown separately where it matters.

    Upgrades are listed as labour only, so you can bring your own SSD or memory, or as supplied, where we source the part. Screen and battery prices depend on the model, which is why some read "from". You get the exact figure for your laptop when we quote.

  • No fix, no fee on data recovery.

    If we cannot get your files back, you do not pay for the attempt.

  • 30-day warranty.

    If the same fault returns within 30 days of collection, the labour to fix it again is on us.
