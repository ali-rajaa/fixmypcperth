Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

FixMyPC Perth (ABN 67 645 153 359) operates from 7 Barraberry Retreat, Canning Vale, Western Australia. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you contact us or book a repair we collect your name, phone number, email address, and the suburb or address relevant to the job. If you send us a message through WhatsApp or our quote form, we keep that conversation so we can follow up on your repair.

When you drop off a device we record its make, model, the fault reported, and any password or PIN you choose to give us so we can test the repair.

Data on your device

This is the part most people care about, so we will be direct about it.

Repairing a computer often requires access to the files on it. Data recovery work requires it by definition. Our technicians access only what is needed to diagnose, repair and verify the work.

We do not copy, retain, share or sell the contents of your device. Where a repair requires us to make a backup, that copy is kept only until you confirm the job is complete and collected, and is then securely deleted. If you want us to keep a backup for longer, tell us and we will agree that in writing.

One exception applies. If we encounter material we are legally obliged to report, we will comply with that obligation. This is rare and we mention it only so the policy is honest.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people visit and how they arrive. It collects an anonymised record of your visit, including approximate location, device type and referring page. It does not identify you personally. You can opt out using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking analytics in your browser.

Our quote form is delivered by Web3Forms, which processes your submission and forwards it to us by email.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only where necessary to complete your repair, for example with a parts supplier when ordering a replacement screen or battery for your specific model, or where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep job records for seven years to meet Australian tax and warranty obligations. Device backups are deleted as described above. Marketing contact details are removed on request.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call or message us and we will action the request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Security

Devices are stored on secured premises. Workshop machines used for data recovery are encrypted and wiped between jobs. Access to customer records is limited to staff who need it.
