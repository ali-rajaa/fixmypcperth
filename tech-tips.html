What these guides are for

Enough to decide what to do next, and no more.

The guides cover the problems we see most: slow computers that cleaning tools never fix, hard drives showing early signs of failure, infections that run quietly for months, and cracked screens that cost less to fix than people expect. Each one explains what is usually behind the problem, how urgent it is and what the fix costs.

They are not step-by-step repair instructions, on purpose. A half-finished DIY repair on a failing drive usually destroys files that could still have been saved. If you already know what you need, see every repair we do, check our flat-rate pricing or get a fixed quote.

Reading about a fault is useful right up until it is not.

If your computer is doing something from these guides, call or message us with what you are seeing. We will tell you what it is likely to be and what it costs.
