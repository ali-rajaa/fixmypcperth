Defaults to "The N causes, by urgency"; signs, repairs and damage guides set their own so the H2 names what they list. Written short_answer, causes (each: id, title, urgency, urgency_label, text, price_key), faqs (each: q and a) Optional quote_service: the service name the quote email is tagged with, from services.yml quote_options values Optional service_page: slug of the service page that sells this job. Linked from the costs section only once its service_links entry in _data/suburbs.yml is built: true. Editorial rule. A guide says what a cause is, why it happens, how urgent it is and what it costs. It never helps the reader diagnose their own machine: no "if it does X, it is Y", no signs that tell one cause from another, no checks, tools, settings or steps. Only testing tells the causes apart. That is also why prices are NOT shown per cause: a price under each cause invites the reader to pick their own and price it. The single cost table names the cause each fix is for, never the services.yml description, which describes symptoms and would hand over a diagnostic sign. Conversion path: quote button in the header, a nudge on every stop-now cause, then the on-page quote form. The form posts to Web3Forms over fetch with the same field names as /quote-request, so enquiries land in the same shape in the inbox.
